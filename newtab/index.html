Kotilo New Tab

A calm new tab page: your bookmarks in a sidebar, with clock, weather, calendar, to-dos, notes, news, Pomodoro and exchange rates. No account, no tracking, no ads.

Features

Everything you glance at, on every new tab

Bookmarks sidebar

Browse folders in place, search all bookmarks and open tabs, add, edit, delete and drag to reorder. Changes go straight to your browser's bookmarks.

Recent and top sites

Recently closed tabs and your most visited sites, one click away.

News

Add any RSS or Atom feed, or just a site address and its feed is found automatically. Filter by source.

Clock and calendar

World clocks; a monthly calendar with ISO week numbers and public holidays for your country.

Weather

Current conditions, hourly and 5-day forecast, in °C or °F.

Focus, to-dos, notes, Pomodoro

A daily focus line, a to-do list, quick notes and a Pomodoro timer with notifications.

Markets

Exchange rates in your own currency with a converter; gold, silver, platinum and palladium; major coins. Optional US stocks and ETFs with your own free Finnhub key.

Quote of the day

A daily quote, with an offline list as a fallback.

Your wallpapers, your settings

Photos or looping videos stored on your device. Settings sync through your browser account; accent colours; show or hide every part.

On first run, the base currency, holiday country and units are guessed from your browser language; change them under Settings → Region and units.

Privacy

Stays in your browser

  • No account, no analytics, no ads. The extension has no server of its own; the developer receives none of your data.
  • Your data stays local. Settings, notes, to-dos and wallpapers live in your browser's extension storage.
  • Only the services it needs. Weather, holidays, exchange rates and the daily quote come straight from public services.
  • Optional sources ask first. Finnhub, regional funds and RSS feeds request permission for that site before the first request.
