Questions. Short answers.

      

        

          Which agents work with Kotilo Desktop?
          
Claude Code, Codex CLI, GitHub Copilot CLI, Gemini CLI, Qwen Code, Factory Droid, OpenCode and Cursor CLI, plus any shell: PowerShell, cmd, Git Bash, WSL, bash and zsh. You can add your own agent with an adapter.json file. The support table lists what works for each.

        

        

          Does Kotilo change my agent's config?
          
Only when you install an agent's integration in Settings. Kotilo backs the file up first, adds only its own hook entries, leaves files it cannot parse alone, and removes its entries again when you uninstall the integration. It never reads your agents' login or token files.

        

        

          What happens when I close the window?
          
Kotilo quits, unless you turn on "Keep running in the tray" under Settings → About & diagnostics. Quitting closes the terminals Kotilo opened, along with the agents running in them, and stops its background service. Agents you started in other terminals keep running. Your workspaces and tabs come back the next time you open Kotilo.

        

        

          Does anything leave my machine?
          
No account, no server, no analytics, in the apps or on this site. Prompts and responses are never written to Kotilo's database or logs. The few connections: the update check (can be turned off), a speech model download when you choose one, AI providers you set up yourself, and, in Deck and New Tab, the weather and other services you turn on. Each product's privacy page lists them: Desktop, Deck, New Tab.

        

        

          Is it free?
          
Yes. Kotilo Desktop is open source under Apache-2.0; Kit, Deck and New Tab under MIT. No paid tier, no account.
