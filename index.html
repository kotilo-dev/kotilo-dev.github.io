How we build

The same rules for every tool

  • Local first. Your settings and data live on your device. No Kotilo account, no Kotilo server.
  • No telemetry. No analytics, crash reporting or tracking in any product, or on this site.
  • Open source. Every product's source is on GitHub under Apache-2.0 or MIT.
  • Few, stated connections. When a tool goes online (weather, updates, feeds you add), its privacy notes list exactly where.
  • Your themes. Catppuccin, Tokyo Night, Nord, Dracula, Gruvbox, Rosé Pine and more in the desktop apps.
  • No ads. Nothing on the screen is there to sell you something.
