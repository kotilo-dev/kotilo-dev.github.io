Kotilo is Finnish for a snail shell. Koti means home.

A desk for your AI coding agents, and a few small tools around it.

Free, open-source tools that run on your own machine: a workspace for AI coding agents, an offline developer toolbox, a dashboard for your second screen and a new tab page.

Kotilo Desktop Source on GitHub

Products

They share the dev themes you already use, and each one works on its own.

Kotilo Desktop

Claude Code, Codex, Copilot, Gemini and more side by side in real terminals, with live agent status, one-click permission answers, worktrees for parallel agents, and usage, cost and plan limits.

Windows · Linux · macOS preview · Apache-2.0

Kotilo Kit

An offline, keyboard-first toolbox: Base64, JSON, JWT, timestamps, regex, hashes and 25 more tools. Clipboard to result in two keystrokes, also from the command line.

Windows · macOS · Linux · CLI · MIT
New Tab
Kotilo New Tab

Your bookmarks in a sidebar on every new tab, with clock, weather, calendar, to-dos, notes, news, Pomodoro and exchange rates.

Chrome Web Store · Firefox Add-ons · MIT
Kotilo Deck

A full-screen dashboard for the small display next to your monitor: clock, weather, calendar, system load, media, pomodoro, to-dos and your AI agents, with approve and deny for Kotilo Desktop.

Windows · Linux · any browser on your network · MIT

On your machine

Everything runs on your machine. No account, no server, no analytics, in the apps or on this site. When a tool does go online (weather, update check), its privacy page says exactly where. Source on GitHub, Apache-2.0 or MIT.
