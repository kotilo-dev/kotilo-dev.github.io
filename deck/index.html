A dashboard for your second display

Kotilo Deck keeps what you glance at all day on a small screen next to your main monitor: clock, weather, calendar, system load, media, volume, a pomodoro timer, to-dos and your AI agents.

Features

Arrange it once, glance at it all day

Ten widgets

Clock, Date, Weather (no API key), Calendar (ICS feeds), System (CPU per core, RAM, GPU, top processes), Volume with a per-app mixer, Media, Pomodoro, Todo and Agent Status.

Workspaces

Saved dashboards with their own layout and theme. Switch with a swipe, the top bar, Ctrl+Alt+←/→, the tray, or rules for time of day, pomodoro start or a calendar event.

Snap-to-grid layout

8×4 in landscape, 4×8 in portrait, adjustable density. Each widget has a few fixed sizes with a layout made for each.

31 themes

Catppuccin, Tokyo Night, Nord, Dracula, Gruvbox, Rosé Pine, Solarized, GitHub, One Dark and more, plus your own. Light/dark switch, wallpapers, transparency and blur.

Companion for Kotilo Desktop

See running agents and approve or deny pending actions from the deck. Kotilo Desktop connects on your machine only.

Tablet mode

Open the deck in any browser on your network, such as an old tablet or phone, by scanning a QR code. Token protected.

Widget plugins

Drop an HTML widget into ~/.kotilo/deck/widgets/. See the plugin API.

Made for always-on screens

Pixel shift, a night dim schedule and idle dim with a screensaver clock. Signed updates, installed only when you click.

Touch and keyboard

Touch first with 44 px targets, fully usable from the keyboard. English and Turkish interface.

Hardware ideas

Any small screen will do

  • A 7" to 15" USB-C portable display next to or below your main monitor. 1024×600 and 1920×1080 are both first-class layouts; one cable carries video and touch.
  • An old tablet or phone in tablet mode: turn it on in Settings and scan the QR code. No app to install.
  • A portrait mount: on a vertical display the grid becomes 4×8 automatically, which suits a monitor arm or a stand beside a laptop.
  • Always on? Turn on pixel shift and the night dim schedule.
Privacy

No account, no cloud, no telemetry

Kotilo Deck only goes online for the weather of the locations you add (Open-Meteo), the calendar feeds you add, and the daily update check on GitHub, which can be turned off. The companion server and tablet mode listen on your machine and local network only. Settings, workspaces and to-dos stay in ~/.kotilo/deck/.
